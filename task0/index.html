Movies

Inception

Inception is a science-fiction thriller about Dom Cobb, a skilled thief who can enter people's dreams and steal their secrets. He is given a difficult mission: instead of stealing an idea, he must plant one inside a person's mind. To complete the mission, Cobb and his team must travel through several layers of dreams, where reality becomes increasingly difficult to distinguish from imagination.

Harry Potter

Harry Potter is a fantasy film series about a young wizard who discovers that he has magical powers and is invited to study at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Along with his friends Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger, Harry faces dangerous adventures, discovers secrets about his past, and fights against the dark wizard Lord Voldemort.

Interstellar

Interstellar is a science-fiction film about a group of astronauts who travel through a mysterious wormhole in search of a new home for humanity. The story follows Cooper, a former pilot and father, who must leave his family behind to explore distant planets. As the mission continues, the crew faces extreme dangers, time differences, and difficult choices about love, family, and the survival of humanity.
